MSc thesis research extending Whittaker & Di Luca (2026), "Constructing extreme heatwave storylines with differentiable climate models." Built a modular Python research package (data, model, optimization, ensemble, evaluation, and plotting) in JAX with a reproducible pipeline over ARCO-ERA5 reanalysis data. Ran NeuralGCM lead-time sensitivity and ensemble experiments on the August 2025 St. John's heatwave and built a heatwave atlas spanning 7 historical events across Köppen climate zones.
