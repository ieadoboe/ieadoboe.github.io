Two internal applications built at the Center for Analytics, Informatics, and Research (CAIR) for Memorial's Research Initiatives & Services (RIS) team. The policy chatbot answers questions grounded in university policies and procedures, with a document ingestion pipeline, A/B tested retrieval and prompt configurations, and a production chat UI. The ticketing system handles internal requests, with a UI built on TailwindCSS and Web Awesome and feature flags to right-size scope. Both apps share a common application core and are containerized (Docker/Podman) and deployed to university VMs with phased testing.
